### Example 1: The Skeptic
> "I signed up thinking this was vaporware. 11 dimensions? Come on. But then I tried the personal knowledge base template and... wait. This actually works. I've been organizing my notes in folders for 20 years. Folders suck. They're always the wrong hierarchy. With coordinates, I just... pick a different angle. Same note, different path to it. **Why it works:** Honest. Specific. Shows the actual moment of understanding.

### Example 2: The Builder
> "I forked the multi-agent template and it broke immediately. Turns out I misunderstood how coordinates reset when you cross delimiters. Spent two hours debugging. Then I got it. Now I have three AI agents writing to shared coordinates and reading each other's output. They're coordinating through *the substrate itself*. No message queue. No API. Just scrolls at known coordinates. This is the weirdest, coolest thing I've built in years."

**Why it works:** Shows the learning curve. Doesn't hide the friction. Celebrates the breakthrough.

### Example 3: The Philosopher
> "I don't know if I'll use SQ Cloud long-term. But I needed to try it because of what it *implies*. If text can be 11-dimensional, what else are we treating as 2D that isn't? Conversations? Relationships? Time itself? Phext is a proof of concept for something bigger. I'm here to see where that leads."

**Why it works:** Gets at the deeper implications. Honest about uncertainty.
