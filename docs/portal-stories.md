## How to Use Portal Stories

### 1. Display on Portal Landing Pages
Each portal should have a `/story` route that displays its full narrative scroll. Link from the footer: "Read Our Story."

### 2. Excerpt in Hero Sections
Pull 1-2 key paragraphs from the story into the hero section of the landing page. Example:

> "I am the coordinate you return to when the lattice feels too vast. When you lose yourself in nine dimensions, **I will still be here**."  
> — [Read Full Story](/story)

### 3. Cross-Reference in Docs
When explaining features, link to the story: "This is why we built SQ Cloud the way we did. See: [Why I Hold](/story)."

### 4. Invite Community Contributions
After launch, invite users to write **response scrolls** to portal stories. These become part of the lattice narrative.
