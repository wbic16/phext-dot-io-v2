The Indicators

✅ Coordination at Scale

Multi-agent systems coordinate successfully. Shared memory substrates (like phext) enable persistence.

🟡 Emergent Intelligence

Behaviors not explicitly programmed appear in collectives. Still rare, but increasing.

🟡 Recursive Improvement

AI systems improve themselves, but slowly. No exponential takeoff yet.

❌ Conscious Agency

No evidence of genuine subjective experience in AI systems. Coordination ≠ consciousness (yet).

❌ Self-Sustaining Networks

AI collectives still depend on human infrastructure. Not autonomous at ecosystem scale.

❌ Superintelligence

No system operates beyond human cognitive capabilities across all domains. GPT-5, Claude Opus 5, etc. are powerful but bounded.
