❌ The Problem with Folders

Folders force you into trees. Where does "Project Alpha Budget Review" go?

  • /Work/Projects/Alpha/?
  • /Finance/Budgets/2026/?
  • /Reviews/Q1/?

You pick one. The other contexts are lost. Tags help, but they're not structure.

✅ The Power of Coordinates

Phext coordinates are multi-dimensional. One note can exist in multiple contexts simultaneously.

2026.Q1.Review/Finance.Budget/Projects.Alpha

Time dimension. Finance dimension. Project dimension. All at once. Navigate by any path.

Example: Daily Journal

2026.2.6/journal.personal/morning.1
This coordinate places your journal entry at:
• Date: Feb 6, 2026
• Category: Personal journal
• Time: Morning (first entry)

Tomorrow: 2026.2.7/journal.personal/morning.1
Evening entry today: 2026.2.6/journal.personal/evening.1
Work journal: 2026.2.6/journal.work/morning.1
